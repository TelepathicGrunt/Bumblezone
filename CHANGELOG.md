### **(V.7.20.0 Changes) (1.20.1 Minecraft)**

#### Dimension:
Fixed the dedicated Bumblezone bee spawning from spawning bes outside world top/bottom and causing bees to teleport out.

#### Items:
A new optional resourcepack is added to this mod to apply 26.1.2 Bumblezone's spawn egg textures.
 You can enable it in-game in resourcepack screen (move it to top of Selected column) or extract it from the mod jar to your resourcepacks folder.
